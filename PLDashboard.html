Which columns does my CSV need?

Required: name, the listed market value (market_value_eur or actual), and the model's estimate (predicted_value_eur or predicted). Optional: club, position, age, goals, assists, minutes. Values can be in euros (45000000) or in millions (45). Column names are not case sensitive.
